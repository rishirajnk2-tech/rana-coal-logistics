Understanding Coal Quality Parameters

Industrial boilers and furnaces require exact combustion chemistry. Consignments undergo proximate analysis to certify energy output and reduce slagging risks.

GCV (Gross Calorific Value): Measures total thermal heat generation per kilogram on an as-received (ARB) or dry-basis (ADB).
Total Moisture (TM): Influences transportation payload costs and furnace ignition temperature thresholds.
Ash & Volatiles: Determines post-combustion residue volume, fly-ash handling, and flame propagation stability.
